Неактивні записи видаляємо через 12 місяців. **Щоб видалити свої дані — надішли боту /delete**, вони зникнуть одразу.

Щоб видалити дані, відклич доступ у налаштуваннях TikTok і напиши нам — ми видалимо токен протягом 7 днів.

## In English

**Website.** No registration, no names or emails collected on the site. Visit statistics use Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless, no cross-site tracking). YouTube embeds load only after a click (youtube-nocookie.com).

**@ShikarnoBaitBot (Telegram bot).** We store your Telegram ID, the name you typed, chosen profession and AI tool, progress, and your **email only if you choose to provide it** (to send your assistant kit and weekly updates). The bot checks whether you are subscribed to @shikarno_ai_ua (Telegram returns only the subscription status). Data is kept in secure Cloudflare storage, accessible only to the ShIkarno team, never sold or shared. Inactive records are deleted after 12 months. **Send /delete to the bot to erase your data immediately.**

**ShIkarno TikTok app.** After the account owner authorizes the app on TikTok, we receive and store only what is needed to upload our own videos: the account identifier (open_id) and an access token. We do not read messages, followers or other account data, and we never share or sell data. Tokens are kept in secure storage, not in public code. To delete your data, revoke access in TikTok settings and contact us — we delete the token within 7 days.
